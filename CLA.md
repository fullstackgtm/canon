# Canon Contributor License Agreement

## Summary

This summary is not part of the agreement. The agreement is the text under
[the agreement](#the-agreement), and that text is what you sign.

Canon is open source. The application is licensed under AGPL-3.0 and the
`engine/` directory under Apache-2.0. Full Stack GTM LLC maintains it. The
company also runs CanonCRM as a hosted service and sells modules that are not
open source, and both include the code in this repository. We ask every outside
contributor to sign this agreement before their first pull request is merged,
so that we can ship your contribution in those products as well as in the
open-source release.

Signing does:

- give Full Stack GTM LLC a permanent, worldwide, royalty-free license to your
  contribution, covering copyright and any of your patents it needs. The
  license includes the right to sublicense it and to distribute it under terms
  other than AGPL-3.0 or Apache-2.0;
- confirm that you have the right to grant that license.

Signing does not:

- transfer your copyright. You still own your contribution and can use it in
  any way you like;
- oblige you to support your contribution, or give any warranty for it;
- change the license under which anyone else receives this repository.

A `Signed-off-by` line (the Developer Certificate of Origin) is not enough here
on its own. It certifies where the code came from, but it grants no license
beyond the repository's own.

**If your employer owns your work.** Many employment contracts give the
employer the rights to code you write, sometimes including code written in your
own time. If yours does, get your employer's written permission to contribute
under this agreement before you sign, and name your employer on an `Employer:`
line in your signature file. We may ask to see that permission. There is no
separate corporate agreement yet. If your employer needs one, open an issue
before you contribute.

[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#before-your-first-pull-request) shows how to
sign.

The agreement is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual
Contributor License Agreement, version 2.2. Full Stack GTM LLC replaces the
Foundation. The Foundation's promise about nonprofit use is removed. Section 2
adds the right to distribute contributions under other license terms. Section 4
refers to the employer's written agreement instead of a corporate agreement.
